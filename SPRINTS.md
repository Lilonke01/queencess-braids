## Problem to validate

Mobile braid appointments depend on the style, time and travel area. The working hypothesis is that collecting those details in one request can reduce follow-up messages and help the owner decide whether to accept a booking.

For Route A, ask the owner for permission, observe how an appointment is currently arranged, and capture what goes wrong and how often. Ask separately for permission to include the establishment contact in the private submission. If using Route B, speak to at least three people with the problem. Record actual observations and feedback in the walkthrough.

## Walkthrough outline

Identify the people affected and explain the current workflow. Show a booking moving from client request to owner decision. Explain why a structured form was chosen, include real feedback or test results, and describe what to build next. Keep the final walkthrough to five minutes or two pages.
